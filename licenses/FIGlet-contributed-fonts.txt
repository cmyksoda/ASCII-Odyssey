FIGlet contributed fonts
========================

The 38 title fonts in vendor/figlet-contributed/ come unmodified from FIGlet's contributed font
collection:

  ftp://ftp.figlet.org/pub/figlet/fonts/contributed.tar.gz
  archive dated 23 Oct 2002, fetched 20 Oct 2026
  sha256 2c569e052e638b28e4205023ae717f7b07e05695b728e4c80f4ce700354b18c8

FIGlet's own license (FIGlet-LICENSE.txt) covers FIGlet and its standard fonts, not these. Each
font belongs to the people named in its header. Most headers give no license terms at all; where a
header does state terms, they are quoted below. None of them restricts redistribution. Each font's
full header, with its authors' contact details, stays intact in its .flf file and in the app.
