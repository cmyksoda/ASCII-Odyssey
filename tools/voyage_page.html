Voyages, {{ROUND}}

The same eight pieces as round four, one for each mood, and all eight sound exactly as they did then. What's new:

  • You gave midnight skyline 10/10. Its music is unchanged, but the new scenes shuffled its pictures a little, and its drops came out a shade calmer, with two hypnotic shots of eight where round four had three.
  • Nine more scenes of the kaleidoscope and checkerboard kind: the Amiga's bouncing ball, a kaleidoscope of gems, twisting tiles, Escher's circle limit flying across a checkered hyperbolic plane, a Sierpinski carpet zooming forever, a quasicrystal rosette, a tunnel seen through a kaleidoscope, two checkerboards turning against each other in xor, and tumbling blocks that turn inside out as the light swings around.
  • Six gentle ones for the soft moods: soap bubbles blown by the tune, a snow globe shaken on the bar, a dandelion letting its seeds go one note at a time, a music box ballerina turning a pirouette every bar, strings of fairy lights, and a mandala the melody draws for itself, a petal for every note.
  • More variety inside the favorites. The kaleidoscope is sometimes set in rose-window tiers that turn against each other, the checker vortex is sometimes a dartboard of counter-turning rings, and the rubber checkerboard's lens sometimes twists into a whirlpool. Truchet tiles are sometimes a 10 PRINT maze, xor checkers sometimes cross a board with rings, the nested squares are sometimes triangles or hexagons, and the checkered box tunnel is sometimes hexagonal or triangular. The honeycomb is sometimes filled in three colors that turn as each kick's ring passes, and the cafe wall's courses sometimes run up the screen, though neither of those last two turns up in these videos.
  • 90 scenes in all, up from 75.
  • 32 kinds of transition, up from 26: tetris blocks, a ripple, a halftone screen of growing dots, a pinwheel, a honeycomb wave, and an old CRT warming up.
  • 60 palettes, up from 47: blueprint, halloween, cga, mesa, mint chip, foundry, twilight, home micro, risograph, goldfish, jade, amber terminal and seafoam. Aurora and snow are now called northern lights and porcelain, since two scenes have those names.
  • 57 title fonts, up from 16. Three more were made for Odyssey (wire, scanline and neon), and 38 come from FIGlet's big contributed collection, as you said yes to. Each mood now picks from fourteen, and a title too wide for one line stacks its words, so the big faces get their turn. They're all below the voyages, so you can keep or drop each one. Each font's makers and their terms are credited in a licenses folder, which the SD card zip carries from 0.2.0 on.
