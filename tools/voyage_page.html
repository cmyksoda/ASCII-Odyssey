Voyages, {{ROUND}}

One voyage for each of the nine moods, with picture and sound together, at 4:3 like your Wii's TV. What changed since the first round:

  • Drops land harder. In the electronic moods a deep sub-bass sits under every drop, and in every mood the rest of the song steps back a little so the drop arrives bigger than anything before it. Drops now run back to back or across a real breakdown, so a short break no longer stalls them.
  • Jungle is built on the Amen break, chopped and edited with stuttering fills, with chord stabs over it.
  • A ninth mood, dubstep, with half-time drums and a wobble bass whose filter moves in time with the beat and changes speed through each bar.
  • 29 scenes instead of 12, and each kind either always answers the music or never does. New ones that answer it include a kaleidoscope, a spectrum analyzer, fireworks, lasers and ripples. New ones that move on their own include clouds, a lava lamp, lanterns, jellyfish, terrain, a galaxy and a moonlit ocean.
  • Shots change about every five to nine seconds, scenes rarely repeat within a piece, and calm music gets calm scenes.
  • Eleven kinds of transition in place of hard cuts: dissolves, wipes, diagonals, a radial burst, an iris, blinds, a checkerboard, a spiral, a melt, a decode and a fade through the ground.
  • 31 palettes plus generated ones, including the rainbow and CMYK you asked for. Each piece gets three related palettes, one for its calm parts, one for its drops and one for builds and breaks. Thunder ribbon plays in rainbow and satellite comet in CMYK.
  • The sunset's floor lines no longer blink out, the hard-to-read sailboat sea is gone, and the star chart fills in.
